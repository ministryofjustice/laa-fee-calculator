# Crown Court Fee Calculation

The language used for calculating fees payable to Crown Court advocates.

## Language

**AGFS fee scheme**:
A schedule of advocate fees. Scheme 18 eligibility depends on the representation-order date, not the main-hearing date.

**Additional claim hearing fee**:
A separate hourly fee for a sentencing hearing or a hearing for mitigation of sentence. In scheme 18 it is independently claimable and can accompany other fees.

**Advocate grade**:
The category of advocate determining the fee rate. Scheme 18's new fees share a rate for junior alone and led junior, with separate leading junior and KC rates.
